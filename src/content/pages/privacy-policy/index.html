Política de Privacidad

Vertical respects your privacy and is committed to explaining how we collect, use, and protect your personal data on this website, as we genuinely care about your information, how it is handled, and ensuring its safety.

Updated:

4 January 2026

Important Information

This privacy policy explains:

  • What data we collectHow we use it

  • How we keep it secure

  • Your rights regarding your data

By using this website, you agree to this policy.

How We Use Your Personal Data

We only use your data when we have a reason to. Most commonly, we use your information to:

  • Respond to your messages or inquiries

  • Send updates or newsletters (only if you subscribe)

  • Improve our website and user experience

  • Maintain site performance and security

We do not sell your data.

We do not use your data for anything unrelated to this website.

DATA SECURITY

We take security seriously.
We use technical and organizational measures to protect your data from:

  • Unauthorized access

  • Loss or misuse

  • Accidental disclosure

However, no internet transmission is 100% secure.

We do our best to keep your information safe.

THE DATA WE COLLECT ABOUT YOU

We may collect, store, and use the following types of personal data:
Identity Data

  • Name or username (if provided)

Contact Data

  • Email address (if provided)

Technical Data

  • Browser type

  • Country or region

  • Usage data to help improve performance

This information helps us understand how visitors use the site.

COOKIES

This site may use cookies which can help:

  • Improve site performance

  • Analyze usage

  • Remember preferences

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. The site will still work, but some features may behave differently.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

We keep your personal data only as long as needed:

  • To respond to you

  • To operate the site

  • To meet any legal obligations

If we no longer need your data, we delete it.

YOUR LEGAL RIGHTS

You have the right to:

  • Request access to your data

  • Request correction of your data

  • Request deletion of your data

  • Withdraw newsletter consent at any time

If you want to exercise any of these rights, contact us.

DATA RETENTION

We may use trusted third-party tools such as:

  • Analytics platforms

  • Email newsletter services

These tools only receive the data needed to perform their function. They are not allowed to use your data for anything else.
